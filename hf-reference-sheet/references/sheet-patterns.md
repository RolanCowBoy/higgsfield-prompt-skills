# Reference-sheet patterns

Adapted from PASSPORT RUSH records 24–26, 65 and 71; not evaluated against generated outputs.

## Turnaround
Inputs: one approved character; front, side and back requested.
Layout: three equally sized full-body panels, common ground line, relaxed standing pose, comparable figure height.
Authority: the character reference supplies identity, costume and style. Panel instructions supply viewpoint only.
Invariant: asymmetrical marks remain on the same anatomical side. A patch on the wearer's left chest does not move to the right just because a view is mirrored.

Do not describe a rear view with visible front facial features. If the back of a costume is unknown, use a conservative continuation and label it as inferred outside the prompt, or request the design if exact construction is essential.

## Expressions
Inputs: approved face; suspicion, surprise and delight.
Keep head scale, crop, hair design, teeth design and baseline proportions.
Specify different readable actions: looking sideways while hiding a smile; reacting to a sudden sound; noticing a welcome discovery.
Allow expressive changes of lids, brows, cheeks and mouth. Do not lock the smile or iris exposure identically in every expression.
For grotesque animation, preserve the design while allowing the requested deformation.

## Mixed sheet
For “different sides, emotions and poses,” a practical layout is:
- one group of three full-body views;
- a separate group of face studies;
- a separate group of full-body action poses.
Choose output size/layout for readability. This is an example, not a required panel count.
If one image is mandatory, clearly assign regions and preserve margins; do not silently output three sheets.

## Object/vehicle
For front three-quarter, overhead and profile, establish the same construction, paint scheme, roof attachment and wheel layout.
Visibility changes with viewpoint: wheels hidden behind the body need not all be visible in every panel. Do not force markings onto surfaces that face away.
A contact shadow can ground each panel without introducing a different scene.

## Output check
Compare distinctive landmarks across all panels. Check missing panels, cropped limbs, accidental outfit variants, mirrored asymmetry, and uneven figure scales. If only one view fails, retain approved views and repair the failed one when the workflow supports it.
